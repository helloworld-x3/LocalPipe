How to play

Tap a piece, then a highlighted destination. Players alternate turns on this device. Red starts.

  • General: one orthogonal step inside the palace. Generals may never face on an open file.
  • Advisor: one diagonal step in the palace.
  • Elephant: two diagonal steps; its eye must be clear. It cannot cross the river.
  • Horse: one straight step, then one diagonal step. The first step must be clear.
  • Chariot: any clear orthogonal distance.
  • Cannon: moves like a chariot; captures over exactly one intervening piece.
  • Soldier: one step forward; after crossing the river, may also move sideways. Never backward.

Check must be answered. Checkmate or having no legal moves (stalemate) loses the game. Repetition and tournament draw adjudication are not automated in v1.
